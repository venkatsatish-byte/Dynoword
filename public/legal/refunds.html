Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short
  • Try Dynoword free for 7 days. No card is needed, and you won’t be charged automatically when the trial ends.
  • Cancel any time from inside the app, and keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for.
  • Changed your mind after your first payment? Ask within 14 days for a full refund.

Free trial

Every new account gets 7 days free. We don’t ask for payment details during the trial. When it ends, your documents become read-only until you choose to subscribe for US$5.00 per month.

How to cancel

  1. Open Dynoword and go to File → Account, or tap the account icon at the top right.
  2. Choose Manage billing. This opens Stripe’s secure billing page.
  3. Choose Cancel subscription.

You can also email [contact email] and we will cancel for you. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period and is not renewed. After that your documents become read-only, and you can still open and export them.

Refunds

  • First payment: if you ask within 14 days of your first subscription payment, we will refund it in full, no questions asked.
  • Later monthly renewals are not refunded, because you can cancel any time before the next renewal. We don’t give partial refunds for unused days in a month.
  • Mistakes: if you were charged after cancelling, charged twice, or charged because of an error on our side, we will refund the incorrect charge.
  • Service problems: if Dynoword was unavailable or seriously broken for a significant part of a billing period, contact us and we will refund that period.

To request a refund, email [contact email] from the email address on your account. We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under consumer law. If you live in the UK or EU, you have 14 days from your first subscription payment to withdraw from the contract. Our first-payment refund above covers this in full.
