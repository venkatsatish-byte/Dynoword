Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short

  • For your account we keep only your email address, a user ID, your trial start date and your Stripe customer ID.
  • Stripe handles payments, so we never see your card number.
  • We use no ads, no advertising cookies and no analytics, and we don’t sell your data.

3. What stays on your device

Dynoword stores these in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage) on your own device. They are not sent to us:

  • your documents, their settings and any pasted job descriptions;
  • preferences such as theme, zoom and whether the ruler is shown;
  • your sign-in session (managed by Firebase), a cached copy of your plan status so the app works offline, and while you are signing in, the email address the link was sent to.

Deleting the app’s site data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes these from that device. Dynoword does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where UK or EU data protection law applies, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses offered by those providers.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account record (email, user ID, trial date, Stripe customer ID): while your account exists, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete your account.
  • Payment and invoice records: kept by Stripe, and by us where needed, for as long as tax and accounting laws require (often 6–10 years).
  • Hosting and function logs: kept by Netlify for a short period under its own retention settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, or object to or restrict its use. Email [contact email] from the address on your account and we will reply within one month. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time links, so there are no passwords to steal. Our server checks every request against a signed sign-in token, and card details are handled only by Stripe. Because your documents are stored on your device, keep your device and its accounts secure.

9. Children

Dynoword is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell account holders by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
