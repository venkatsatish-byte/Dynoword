    About Dynoword

    An independent, open-source word processor that runs in your browser and installs like a native app.

    • Windows / Mac: open in Edge or Chrome, then choose Install Dynoword from the address bar.
    • iPhone / iPad: open in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    • Android: open in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Install app.
    • Works offline. Documents are stored privately on your device.
